### CHEM-000395: Resolving inverse trans influence in a Pa(V) solution complex
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Experimentally distinguish trans and cis Pa–Cl distances in the mono-oxo chloride complex. Test the predicted inverse trans influence independently of fitting a calculated geometry.
- **Status:** The 2026 analysis predicts trans-bond shortening of 0.04–0.08 Å. That difference falls within the EXAFS uncertainty and was not resolved experimentally.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests a directional-bonding prediction for an exceptionally data-poor actinide.
- **Key works:** M. Maloubier et al. (2026; online 2025), “Persistent Mono-Oxo Bonding with Protactinium(V) Revealed in Highly Acidic Chloride Solutions,” [DOI: 10.1002/anie.202518403](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202518403).
- **Related:** Inverse problems—resolving small structural differences without imposing the answer through the model.
